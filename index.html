Social / Environmental Issue

Plastic pollution starts in the bathroom

Single-use bottles and personal care waste are a growing burden on Taiwan’s coastlines, rivers, and farmland. Small daily habits add up — but so can small changes.

Blueseeds already champions natural farming, biodegradable ingredients, and sustainable production. Partnering with RE-THINK extends that mission from product to participation: education, refill culture, and hands-on community care.

Campaign Concept

Plant the Future:
Return Beauty to Nature

A nationwide PR campaign encouraging consumers to reduce plastic waste while reconnecting with Taiwan’s natural environment through sustainable lifestyle habits.

  • Environmental education

    Workshops and talks that make waste visible — and solutions practical.

  • Refill culture

    Nationwide refill stations and bottle-return rewards across partner stores.

  • Community participation

    Beach and mountain cleanups with volunteers of every age and background.

  • Social storytelling

    Share your green journey with #PlantTheFuture and inspire others to act.

  • Hands-on eco experiences

    Planting herbs, pledge walls, and pop-up labs that turn learning into doing.

🍃

Our 3 Key Actions

🍃
01

Bring Back Your Bottle

Return your empty Blueseeds bottles to partner stores. Earn rewards, support donations, and keep plastic in the loop — not in the ocean.

02

Clean Up, Green Up

Join beach and mountain cleanups and hands-on eco activities across Taiwan. Together for cleaner oceans, healthier land, and a brighter future.

03

One Bottle = One Herb

Every purchase supports local herb farmers and land restoration. Scan the QR on your bottle to track the herb planted in your name.
